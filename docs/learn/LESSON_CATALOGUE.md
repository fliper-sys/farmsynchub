# FarmSync Learning Academy — Expanded Curriculum

**383 lessons · 2034 question records · 54 practical activities**

## Learning pathways

### Crop Production & Horticulture
Beginner: seed quality, germination, nursery and transplanting. Intermediate: crop-specific establishment, crop care, pollination and harvest. Advanced: integrated pest management, field trials, diagnosis and yield interpretation.

### Soil, Water & Climate Resilience
Beginner: soil observation, mulch, compost and moisture. Intermediate: drainage, erosion, water harvesting and seasonal calendars. Advanced: soil sampling, nutrient budgets, irrigation audits and climate risk.

### Livestock, Poultry & Aquaculture
Beginner: housing, feed, water and daily observation. Intermediate: flock/herd routines, welfare and biosecurity. Advanced: production KPIs, isolation, water quality and veterinary escalation.

### Postharvest, Processing & Food Safety
Beginner: clean harvest, shade and sorting. Intermediate: drying, grading, packaging and cold-chain. Advanced: aflatoxin prevention, traceability, mock recall and validated processing.

### Farm Business, Finance & Markets
Beginner: records, costs and buyer checks. Intermediate: inventory, labour, contracts and cooperative sales. Advanced: unit economics, cash-flow stress tests, insurance records and technology appraisal.

### Farm Operations, Safety & Digital Skills
Beginner: tools and task briefings. Intermediate: PPE, heat safety, mapping and backups. Advanced: machinery, chemical inventory, emergency response and continuity planning.

## Assessment standard
- Each Round 4 lesson has six knowledge-check questions with explanations, seven practical steps and four topic-appropriate tools.
- Suggested benchmark: 80% quiz score plus one completed practical activity; this is curriculum guidance and does not alter the app scoring provider.
- Practical evidence can be a dated note, photo, measurement, calculation or completed checklist.
- Advanced learners should explain evidence, risk, cost and why they chose an action.
- High-risk decisions require referral to qualified local extension, veterinary, engineering, food-safety or regulatory support.
