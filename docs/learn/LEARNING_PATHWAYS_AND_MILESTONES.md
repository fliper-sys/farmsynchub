# Learning pathways and suggested milestones

These are curriculum recommendations only. The current static lesson model does not enforce prerequisites or automatically award these new milestone badges.

## Crop Production & Horticulture
Beginner: seed quality, germination, nursery and transplanting. Intermediate: crop-specific establishment, canopy, pollination and harvest. Advanced: IPM, soil diagnosis, nutrient budgets, field trials and yield interpretation.
Milestones: Seed Starter (5 foundation lessons); Field Observer (3 scouting records); Crop Planner (one crop calendar); Trial Builder (one fair field comparison).

## Soil, Water & Climate Resilience
Beginner: soil observation, mulch, compost and moisture. Intermediate: drainage, erosion, water harvesting and seasonal planning. Advanced: representative soil tests, irrigation uniformity, water budgets and agroforestry.
Milestones: Soil Steward (5 lessons); Water Watcher (water-source audit); Resilience Planner (contingency plan).

## Livestock, Poultry & Aquaculture
Beginner: housing, feed, water and daily observation. Intermediate: brooding, welfare, grazing and visitor biosecurity. Advanced: feed quality, isolation, cold-chain records, fish health and outbreak escalation.
Milestones: Daily Care Champion (7 observations); Biosecurity Builder (farm audit); Production Analyst (batch KPI review).

## Postharvest, Processing & Food Safety
Beginner: clean harvest, shade and sorting. Intermediate: drying, grading, packaging and cold chain. Advanced: aflatoxin prevention, traceability, packhouse workflow and validated processing.
Milestones: Quality Keeper (grading exercise); Loss Detective (measure losses at 3 stages); Traceability Ready (mock trace-back).

## Farm Business, Finance & Markets
Beginner: records, receipts and market checks. Intermediate: cashbook, stock, labour and contracts. Advanced: unit economics, cash-flow stress test, insurance documentation and technology appraisal.
Milestones: Record Keeper (7 days of records); Market Analyst (3 buyer offers); Business Planner (downside cash-flow scenario).

## Farm Operations, Safety & Digital Skills
Beginner: tools, task briefings and phone records. Intermediate: PPE, heat safety, mapping and backup. Advanced: chemical inventory, machinery defects, emergency drills and continuity.
Milestones: Safe Operator (pre-start check); Digital Keeper (restore test); Prepared Farm (continuity drill).

## Assessment model
1. Recall: multiple-choice questions with explanations. 2. Application: field or business checklist. 3. Evidence: measurement, photo, log or calculation. 4. Reflection: what changed and what comes next. 5. Escalation: identify when professional or regulatory advice is needed.

Suggested benchmark: 80% quiz score plus a completed practical activity. Existing scoring and certificate logic are unchanged.
